Published a dataset that was curated in collaboration between the Computer Science and Engineering Department, University of Dhaka and the National Institute of Neuroscience, Bangladesh. It comprises 5,285 T1-weighted contrast-enhanced brain MRI images belonging to 38 categories. Available here [Brain MRI Dataset](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.14778750.v2).
